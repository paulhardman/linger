Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Linger collects no data at all. Your audio never leaves your iPhone.

Your audio

Linger changes the speed of audio files entirely on your device. Files you open are copied into the app's temporary storage so they can be played and processed, and are deleted when you open a different file or when iOS clears temporary storage. Linger never uploads, transmits or shares your audio. A processed file only leaves the app when you choose to share or save it yourself.

Data collection

Linger does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. It makes no network connections.

Settings

Linger remembers the last speed you chose. That setting is stored only on your device.

Children

Because Linger collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions about this policy? See the support page.
