Linger

Linger slows down or speeds up audio without changing its pitch. It's useful for podcasts, voice memos, language practice and learning music by ear.

How to use it

  1. Tap Choose an audio file, or share an audio file to Linger from Files, Voice Memos, Mail or another app.
  2. Pick a speed from 70% to 130%. Press play to hear it right away.
  3. Tap Process & Share to save the new file or send it to another app.

Common questions

Which files work?
MP3, M4A, WAV, Opus and most other audio formats iOS can play. The processed file is always saved as M4A.

Why can't I open a song from Apple Music?
Songs from Apple Music are copy-protected, so other apps can't open them.

Does Linger upload my audio?
No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
