## Description (max 4000)

```
Linger slows down or speeds up any audio file without changing its pitch, so voices still sound like themselves and music stays in tune.

Slow a fast talker down to 70% to catch every word, or speed up a long recording to 130% to get through it faster. Then save the new file or send it anywhere.

GREAT FOR
• Language learning: slow down native speakers until you can follow along
• Learning music by ear: hear fast passages note by note, still in the right key
• Podcasts, lectures and interviews: speed up the slow parts or slow down the dense ones
• Voice memos and meeting recordings: make them easier to transcribe

HOW IT WORKS
• Open a file, or share one to Linger from Files, Voice Memos, Mail or Messages
• Drag the speed slider from 70% to 130%. Playback changes as you drag, so you hear the result instantly
• Tap Process & Share to save a new copy, named so you can tell it apart (for example "interview-75-slower.m4a")

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Linger works entirely on your iPhone. Your audio is never uploaded, there's no account to create, and the app collects no data at all.

Works with MP3, M4A, WAV, Opus and most other audio formats.
```
